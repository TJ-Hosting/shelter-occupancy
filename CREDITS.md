## Images

- **Served since shelter-03 (8 Nov 2026): one image.** The hero, the embed card, the 404 page and the share image use the noir title backdrop of [Homeless in Toronto](https://torontohomeless.com/), TJ-Hosting's own AI-generated art (GPT Image via codex exec, 28 Oct 2026; painted Toronto skyline at night, no people). Two crops, both live on the game: `public/assets/ui/backdrop-entry-wide.webp` (1664×936) and `backdrop-entry-tall.webp` (941×1672), copied from `TJ-Hosting/homeless` at `2414eae` and kept here as same-size JPEG masters `assets/pack/sh-hero-noir.jpg` and `sh-hero-noir-tall.jpg`. Their ledger lines, with the game's prompts, ledger ids and hashes, are the last two in [`assets/pack/LEDGER.jsonl`](assets/pack/LEDGER.jsonl); the game's own ledger marks their art approval `pending`. They are the higher-resolution versions of the 1280 px title poster (`loop-title-noir-01-poster.webp`, a frame of the game's title loop made from the same still).
- **Shipped files:** listed with source, ledger line and alt text in [`assets/img/MANIFEST.json`](assets/img/MANIFEST.json); `scripts/build_images.py` converts them to AVIF/WebP, never wider than the source, and `scripts/build_og.mjs` renders the share image.
- **No longer referenced (kept on disk):** the 25-image pre-generated pack made on 2026-10-15 by agent=claude for TJ with Grok Imagine, and the 3 laptop additions of 17 Oct 2026 (Codex): `sh-hero-islands-dusk-hd`, `sh-hero-islands-sunset-hd`, `sh-sc-doorway-v2`. Their converted files stay in `assets/img/` until Barney rules on deleting them (shelter-02 Q2); their ledger lines and full prompts stay in `assets/pack/LEDGER.jsonl`, notes in [`assets/pack/README.md`](assets/pack/README.md).
- **Generated by this project:** 0 images in shelter-01, shelter-02 and shelter-03 (cap: 40 calls, US$10; requests from shelter-01 are in [`assets/REQUESTS.md`](assets/REQUESTS.md)). Any future generated image gets a line in `assets/LEDGER.jsonl`.
- The image is stylised atmosphere, not a photograph and not data; it shows no person.
